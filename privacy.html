Privacy Policy

Soul Vibe Space · Limassol, Cyprus · Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Soul Vibe Space (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you visit our website, book a class, attend an event, or otherwise interact with us. It is provided in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Cyprus law.

1. Who we are (Data Controller)

The data controller is Soul Vibe Space, a yoga and wellness studio based in Limassol, Cyprus. For any privacy-related question or request, contact us at soulvibespace@gmail.com.

Studio address: Agias Zonis, Municipality of Limassol, CY, 3090. Phone: +357 95 642 888.

2. What data we collect

We collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Identification & contact — name, email, phone number
  • Account data — username, encrypted password, language preference
  • Booking data — classes booked, attendance history, packages and memberships
  • Payment data — handled by our secure payment provider (Stripe); we do not store full card numbers
  • Health information — only information you voluntarily share with us (injuries, pregnancy, conditions) to ensure safe participation
  • Consent records — your acceptance of these Terms, Privacy Policy and marketing consent, with timestamp, document version and language
  • Technical data — IP address, device type, browser, pages visited (analytics)

3. Why we use your data and legal basis

We process your personal data for these purposes:

  • To manage your account, bookings and attendance — performance of a contract
  • To ensure safe participation in classes — consent (health data) and vital interests
  • To send service messages (booking confirmations, schedule changes, cancellations) — legitimate interest and contract performance
  • To send marketing emails (news, offers, events) — consent, which you give by ticking the marketing checkbox and can withdraw at any time
  • To improve our services and website — legitimate interest
  • To comply with legal obligations (tax, accounting) — legal obligation

4. Consent at registration

When you create an account or book a class, we ask you to confirm two things:

  • A required consent that you have read and agree to our Terms & Conditions and acknowledge this Privacy Policy.
  • An optional consent to receive newsletters, promotions, and updates from Soul Vibe Space by email.

Both checkboxes are unchecked by default. We store the date, time, version of the documents and language in which you gave consent, so we can demonstrate your choice if asked. You can withdraw your marketing consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email or by writing to soulvibespace@gmail.com.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with trusted service providers acting as data processors on our behalf:

  • SimplyBook.me — booking and CRM platform (EU)
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Google — Google Sign-In and analytics (only if you choose to sign in with Google)
  • Email service providers — to deliver transactional and marketing emails
  • Hosting providers (Netlify, Render) for our website and backend

All processors are bound by data processing agreements and GDPR-compliant safeguards.

6. International transfers

Some of our service providers may be located outside the EEA. In such cases we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards to protect your data.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Account and booking data — for as long as your account is active, plus up to 3 years after last activity
  • Accounting records (invoices, payments) — 6 years, as required by Cyprus law
  • Marketing consent records — until you withdraw consent
  • Health information — only as long as needed for safe participation; deleted when no longer relevant

8. Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (dataprotection.gov.cy)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at soulvibespace@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies and analytics

Our site uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for login and booking to work
  • Preference cookies — store your language and theme choices
  • Analytics cookies — anonymous statistics about how the site is used, to improve it

You can control or refuse non-essential cookies through your browser settings and through our cookie banner.

10. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), secured access to our backend systems, and limited staff access on a need-to-know basis.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will inform you by email or via a notice on the website. The latest version with its date is always available on this page.
