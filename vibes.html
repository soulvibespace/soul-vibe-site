More about VIBES

Gifts for a friend and your birthday

Co-working
Between classes, co-working is free if you have more than %%class%% VIBES. Otherwise, co-working rates apply.
Where can I see my balance?
In your personal account: current VIBES balance, your Class Passes with expiry dates and the history of credits and visits.
Do VIBES expire?
VIBES from a Class Pass or Starter Pack are valid for its period, which starts from your first visit. Gift VIBES never expire.
What if I don’t have enough VIBES?
Top up your balance at the studio by any amount or pay for the class at the studio without using VIBES.
All VIBES rules
  • Validity of a Class Pass starts from your first visit, not from the purchase date.
  • Order of use: VIBES are taken from the earliest purchase first.
  • Top-up: any amount at the studio, %%topup%% = 1 VIBE.
  • Freeze: on request in your personal account, up to %%freeze%% days.
  • Personal: a Class Pass is not intended to be transferred to other people. To give a class as a present, choose a gift certificate.
  • Cancellation: please cancel your booking in advance. No penalty while online payment is not available.
