  • Top-up: any amount at the studio, %%topup%% = 1 VIBE.

Questions & answers

What are VIBES?
VIBES is your practice currency at Soul Vibe Space: a balance you use to pay for classes. A regular class costs %%class%% VIBES; special events have their own price.
How do I start using VIBES?
Register on the website and confirm your email — your VIBES balance is created automatically. VIBES appear on it after you buy a Class Pass or a Starter Pack, or top up at the studio. You can also come without a Class Pass — a single visit costs %%dropin%%.
Do VIBES expire?
VIBES from a Class Pass or Starter Pack are valid for its period, which starts from your first visit. Gift VIBES never expire.
Can I give or transfer VIBES?
A Class Pass is personal and is not intended to be transferred to other people. To give a class as a present, choose a gift certificate.
What if I don’t have enough VIBES?
Pay the difference at the studio or top up your balance: when you top up, %%topup%% becomes 1 VIBE. With a new Class Pass, each class costs less.
Where can I see my balance?
In your personal account: current VIBES balance, your Class Passes with expiry dates and the history of credits and visits.
How do I freeze my Class Pass?
Submit a request in your personal account — the administrator reviews it. A freeze lasts up to %%freeze%% days, and the validity is extended by the frozen days.
