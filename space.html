The studio

Where practice comes alive

Our main studio space is designed to support a wide range of movement and mindfulness practices — from quiet morning yoga to energetic ecstatic dance and immersive sound baths.

High ceilings, natural light, and quality flooring create the perfect environment for your practice. The space can accommodate group classes, private sessions, and special events.

Co-working

Work mindfully

Between classes, our co-working area offers a calm, inspiring environment to focus, create, or connect with others in the community.

With a coffee point, SMART TV, and fast Wi-Fi, it's everything you need to be productive — in a space that feels completely different from a regular office.
