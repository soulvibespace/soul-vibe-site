Questions & Answers

Everything about VIBES, Class Passes and payment.

Can I buy a Starter Pack more than once?
No. A Starter Pack is for new clients and can be purchased only once — choose SP1, SP2 or SP4. After that, Class Passes and top-ups are available.
Can I top up a small amount?
Yes — any amount, at the rate €1 = 1 VIBE.
How much is a special class?
Special classes, workshops and events have their own price in VIBES, set separately for each one. Check the schedule or ask the administrator.
Can I come without a Class Pass?
Of course. A single visit to a regular class without a Class Pass costs €20, paid at the studio.
Can I pay online?
Not yet. Payment is made at the studio, and the administrator confirms it. VIBES then appear on your balance.
Can I get a refund for unused VIBES?
Refunds for unused VIBES are possible at the studio’s discretion, at the rate of 1 VIBE = €0.75. Bonus VIBES (for referring a friend, a review or your birthday) are not refundable.
