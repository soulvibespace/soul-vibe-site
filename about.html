About us

A home for practice, sound and community in Limassol

We created Soul Vibe Space as a truly multifunctional wellness space in Limassol — one home for many practices, teachers and communities.

From gentle morning yoga to Ecstatic Dance, from sound healing and qigong to mindful co-working — every class here is designed to help you feel more present, connected and alive.

The studio is open every day from 8:00 to 22:00. Beginners and experienced practitioners are equally welcome — we will help you find a practice that suits you.
