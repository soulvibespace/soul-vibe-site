About Soul Vibe Space

Soul Vibe Space is a yoga and wellness studio in Limassol offering yoga classes, meditation, ecstatic dance and sound healing.

A space born from intention

We created Soul Vibe Space as a response to the need for a truly multifunctional wellness space in Limassol — a place that brings together diverse practices, teachers, and communities under one roof.

From gentle morning yoga to ecstatic dance, from sound healing sessions to mindful co-working — everything at Soul Vibe Space is designed to help you feel more present, connected, and alive.

Located at 50 Agia Zonis, Limassol, our studio is open every day from 9 am to 10 pm. We welcome beginners and experienced practitioners alike.
